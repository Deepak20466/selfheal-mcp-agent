update_fee applies the payload fields with setattr but never recalculates balance_amount when amount or product_amount changes. Creating a fee sets balance_amount = amount + product_amount (125). Changing amount to 150 left the balance at 125 instead of 175. The fix records the old total before applying updates. If amount or product_amount changed, balance_amount was not set explicitly, and the fee is not PAID, the balance is shifted by the change in total. This keeps any partial payments already reflected in the balance, and the balance is clamped at zero.
