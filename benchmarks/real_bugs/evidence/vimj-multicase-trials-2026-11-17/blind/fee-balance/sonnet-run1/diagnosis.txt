update_fee only applied the submitted fields. When amount or product_amount changed on an unpaid fee without an explicit balance_amount, balance_amount kept its old value (125). The test expects 175 after amount goes from 100 to 150 with product_amount 25. The fix records the old total and old status before applying updates. If the fee was not PAID and the total changed with no explicit balance_amount, the balance shifts by the change in total, so any partial payments are kept. The balance is floored at 0. Fees that were PAID are left alone, and the existing status-update branch still handles those.
