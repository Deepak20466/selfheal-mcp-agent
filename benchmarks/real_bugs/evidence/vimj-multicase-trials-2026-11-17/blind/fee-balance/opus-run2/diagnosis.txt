update_fee() only recomputes balance_amount when the request includes balance_amount or status. When only amount (or product_amount) changes on an unpaid fee, the balance stays at its old value (125) instead of following the new total (175). Fix: record the old total before applying the updates. If amount/product_amount changed, balance_amount wasn't explicitly provided, and the fee isn't PAID, shift the balance by the change in the total (never below 0). This keeps any part-payment already made and leaves all other paths unchanged.
