Root cause: in `update_fee`, the payload fields are blindly copied onto the StudentFee with `setattr`, but `balance_amount` is never recalculated when `amount` changes. The fee was created with balance = amount + product_amount = 125. Updating amount to 150 sets amount=150 and leaves balance_amount at 125, so the outstanding balance is out of sync (expected 175 = 150 + 25).

Fix: capture the previous amount before applying updates. After applying them, if `amount` was supplied without an explicit `balance_amount`, and the fee is not PAID, shift the outstanding balance by the difference (new amount - old amount). Using the delta rather than recomputing amount + product_amount keeps any already-recorded reduction of the balance intact. Paid fees keep their zero balance. Behaviour for every other update path (explicit balance_amount, status changes, product_amount-only edits) is unchanged.
