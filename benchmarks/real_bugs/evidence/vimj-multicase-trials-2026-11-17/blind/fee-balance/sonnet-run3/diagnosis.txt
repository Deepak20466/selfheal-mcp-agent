update_fee applies the payload fields with setattr but never adjusts balance_amount when amount or product_amount changes. Only an explicit balance_amount or status update touched the balance. After raising amount from 100 to 150 on an unpaid fee (product 25), balance_amount stayed at 125 instead of 175. The fix records the old total before applying the updates. If amount or product_amount changed, balance_amount was not supplied, and the fee was not already PAID, it shifts the outstanding balance by the change in total (floored at 0). Paid fees keep their zero balance, and the existing status/balance branches are unchanged.
