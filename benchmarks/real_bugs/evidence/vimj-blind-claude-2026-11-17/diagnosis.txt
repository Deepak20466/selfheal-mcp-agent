update_fee copies the payload fields onto the fee but never touches balance_amount when only amount or product_amount changes. The balance therefore keeps the old outstanding value (125) instead of tracking the new total (150 + 25 = 175). The fix records the old total before applying the updates. If amount or product_amount changed, and neither balance_amount nor status was supplied, and the fee isn't already PAID, it shifts the balance by the change in total, floored at 0.
